How to play

Knock every can off its feet before you run out of balls.

  1. Drag back to aim

    Press anywhere and pull down. Pull further to throw higher; move sideways to aim left or right. The dotted line shows the path.

  2. Release to throw

    Let go to launch. Drag back to where you started to cancel.

  3. Clear the stand

    A can counts once it falls off or tips over. Knock several with one ball for combo points.

  4. Next level

    Knock down every can to complete the level and unlock the next one. Balls you don't use add bonus points.
